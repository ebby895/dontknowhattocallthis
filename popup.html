Last updated: December 13, 2025

1. Overview

AutoList Pro is a productivity and automation helper designed to assist users in managing Facebook Marketplace listings more efficiently. AutoList Pro operates on top of Facebook’s web interface and does not modify, access, or integrate directly with Facebook or Meta systems.

AutoList Pro is an independent tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or partnered with Facebook or Meta Platforms, Inc.

2. Facebook Account Responsibility

AutoList Pro does not manage, authenticate, or verify your Facebook login.

All actions performed by AutoList Pro occur on the Facebook account currently signed into your browser at the time of use. You are solely responsible for:

  • Which Facebook account is logged in
  • The content of listings created or modified
  • Compliance with Facebook Marketplace policies and Terms of Service

AutoList Pro does not determine whether you are authorized to post on Facebook Marketplace.

3. Credentials & Data Access

AutoList Pro:

  • Does not collect Facebook usernames or passwords
  • Does not read Facebook cookies
  • Does not bypass Facebook authentication
  • Does not transmit Facebook login data to external servers

Authentication for AutoList Pro itself (if enabled) is handled separately and does not grant access to your Facebook credentials.

4. Automation & Platform Changes

AutoList Pro relies on Facebook’s publicly available web interface. As a result:

  • Automation features may break, change behavior, or stop working at any time
  • Facebook updates, rate limits, or enforcement actions may impact functionality
  • AutoList Pro cannot guarantee uninterrupted or error-free operation

You acknowledge that automation carries inherent risk.

5. User Responsibility

You are fully responsible for:

  • Listings created, edited, deleted, or reposted
  • Pricing, descriptions, images, and compliance
  • Consequences resulting from automation use
  • Any account actions taken by Facebook, including warnings, limits, or bans

AutoList Pro is a tool, not a decision-maker.

6. Data Storage

AutoList Pro may store user data locally on your device and/or remotely (such as cloud storage) to support functionality.

When multi-user support is enabled:

  • Data is logically separated per user
  • Logging out and logging in with a different account will load that user’s data

AutoList Pro does not sell user data.

To the maximum extent permitted by law, AutoList Pro shall not be liable for:

9. Termination & Uninstall

Local Backup Folder

Not connected — listings save to the extension only.
Every time you save a listing, a copy (details + photos + videos) is written to this folder as plain files — separate from the extension's own storage. Once a folder is connected, use the "📤 Export Listings…" button in the Saved Listings toolbar any time to copy your saved listings there — a window will pop up letting you pick which ones. Use "Import All From Folder" to pull listings from that folder back into the extension — existing listings are left untouched, only ones not already here get added. A file named activity_log.txt in that folder keeps a simple written record of what's been exported.
